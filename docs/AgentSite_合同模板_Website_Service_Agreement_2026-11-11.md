## 4. Client Obligations / 客户责任

4.1 The Client shall provide accurate business information, REN/SSM details, logos, images, listing data, contact details and all content required for the Services.

4.2 The Client warrants that it owns or has permission to use all content and images supplied, and that its listings, claims and advertising comply with Malaysian law, including estate agency rules and PDPA.

4.3 The Client shall respond to requests for approval within **3 business days**. Delays extend the delivery timeline.

4.4 The Client shall not upload unlawful, infringing, misleading or malicious content.

4.5 The Client is responsible for maintaining its own domain registrar account where the domain is registered in the Client’s name.

## 5. Delivery and Acceptance / 交付与验收

5.1 The Provider shall use reasonable efforts to deliver the standard site within **1–3 business days** after the later of:

- receipt of the Setup Fee; and
- receipt of complete Client materials.

5.2 The Client shall review the staging site within **5 business days** and notify the Provider in writing of any material non-conformity with Schedule A.

5.3 If no written rejection is received within 5 business days, the site is deemed accepted.

5.4 Bug fixes are included. New requirements, redesigns and additional revisions are Change Requests under Clause 3.4.

5.5 The Provider may publish the site on a temporary subdomain before the Client’s domain is ready; this counts as delivery.

6.2 The Provider configures DNS, SSL and hosting. The Client shall not change DNS records without informing the Provider; the Provider is not responsible for downtime caused by Client DNS changes.

6.3 Hosting is provided on the Provider’s cloud account. The Provider may migrate the site to another server or provider with reasonable notice.

6.4 The Provider does not guarantee uninterrupted availability of third-party services (domain registrar, payment gateway, WhatsApp, cloud provider).

## 8. Maintenance and Support / 维护与支持

8.1 The Subscription Fee includes the maintenance services in **Schedule C**: hosting, SSL, backups, security updates, bug fixes and the stated content updates.

8.2 Maintenance excludes:

- new pages, new features, redesign or new integrations;
- content updates beyond the stated allowance;
- issues caused by Client or third-party modifications;
- third-party outages;
- SEO ranking or sales performance;
- advertising management.

8.3 **SLA:** response times and service credits are set out in Schedule C. Business hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00 Malaysia time, excluding public holidays.

8.4 **Backups:** daily backups with 14-day retention. One restore per year is included; additional restores are RM 150 each.

8.5 **Uptime target:** 99.5% monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance and force majeure. Service credits, if any, are limited to 30% of the monthly Subscription Fee.

> 中文提示：维护边界必须列清楚；否则“帮我改一下”会无限消耗工时。

## 9. Renewal, Suspension and Termination / 续费、暂停与终止

9.1 The Provider shall send renewal reminders at 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before expiry.

9.2 If payment is not received by expiry, the site enters a **7-day grace period**, then may be suspended.

9.3 During suspension, the Client portal remains accessible for payment and data export.

9.4 If payment remains outstanding for **30 days** after suspension, the Provider may delete the site and data after 7 days’ written notice.

9.5 Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 14 days after written notice.

9.6 On termination, the Client may request an export of its content and leads for RM 150. The Provider will delete data after the retention period, unless required by law.

9.7 Prepaid fees are non-refundable on early termination by the Client, except where required by law.

## 10. Data Protection / 个人资料保护

10.1 The parties shall comply with the **Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)**.

10.2 The Client is the data controller of lead data collected through its website. The Provider acts as a data processor and processes such data only to provide the Services.

10.3 The Provider shall implement reasonable security measures, including access control, encryption in transit, backups and confidentiality obligations.

10.4 The Provider shall notify the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Client data.

10.5 The Provider may process technical logs and account data for security, billing and service improvement, in accordance with its privacy policy.

10.6 The Client is responsible for obtaining consent for marketing communications and for its own lead follow-up.

> 中文提示：PDPA 下客户通常是控制者，服务商是处理者；建议另附 DPA（Schedule D）。
